PROJECT 01 / DEEP AWARENESS

Deep Awareness.

A calm and immersive digital identity created for a therapist working across hypnotherapy and psychology.

WEB DESIGN / DEVELOPMENT / IDENTITY PORTUGAL / 2026
01

A digital space built around calm, trust & connection.

Deep Awareness is a digital identity and website designed for a therapist working across hypnotherapy and psychology.

The goal was to create an online space where visitors could understand the therapeutic practice, discover the available services and easily move toward booking a consultation.

The experience was intentionally designed to feel welcoming and reflective rather than clinical or overwhelming.

02 / PROCESS

Designing from feeling first, structure second.

The process started by defining how the website should make someone feel before deciding how it should look. Calm, clarity and emotional safety became the foundation for the entire visual and digital system.

01 / DISCOVERY

Understand

Define the therapist's approach, audience and the information visitors need before feeling comfortable enough to make contact.

02 / DIRECTION

Translate

Transform ideas of introspection, trust and calm into an astral visual language built from deep colour, circular forms and soft contrast.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Guide

Build a clear path through the website: understand the practice, learn about the therapist, explore services and move naturally toward booking.

03 / STORYTELLING

Turning a biography into a journey.

Instead of using a conventional biography, the therapist's background is presented as a visual timeline.

This creates a more human way of showing her path, education and development while maintaining the quiet rhythm of the site.

04

An astral identity designed around stillness.

01 / COLOUR

Deep tones

Dark navy creates depth and stability, while soft gold introduces warmth, guidance and an understated astral quality.

02 / TYPOGRAPHY

Soft contrast

Large serif typography creates emotional presence while restrained supporting text keeps information clear and easy to absorb.

03 / MOTION

Slow rhythm

Subtle motion, circular forms and generous spacing encourage slower navigation and support a feeling of reflection.

05 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

Quiet, spacious and focused.

Circular elements reference cycles, introspection and balance. Combined with large areas of negative space, they reinforce the feeling of calm throughout the interface.

06

A website designed to turn understanding into connection.

The final experience gives visitors a clear and calm path through the therapist's work without reducing the service to a purely commercial journey.

Information, emotional tone and consultation booking are integrated into one coherent digital experience.

01 / TRUST

Clearer presence

A more intentional digital identity that communicates professionalism without feeling clinical.

02 / CLARITY

Easier navigation

Services, background and therapeutic approach are presented through a clear visual hierarchy.

03 / ACTION

Simple booking path

Visitors can move naturally from learning about the practice to making contact or booking a consultation.
