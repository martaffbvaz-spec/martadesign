PROJECT 01 / DEEP AWARENESS

Deep Awareness.

A calm and immersive digital identity created for a therapist working across hypnotherapy and psychology.

WEB DESIGN / DEVELOPMENT / IDENTITY PORTUGAL / 2026
01

A digital space designed to feel calm, trustworthy & human.

Deep Awareness is a digital identity and website created for a therapist working across hypnotherapy and psychology.

The goal was to build a calm and trustworthy online space where people could understand the practice, explore the available services and easily book consultations.

The experience was designed to feel welcoming rather than clinical, creating a sense of stillness from the very first interaction.

02 / THE GOAL

Creating calm before action.

The website needed to provide enough information for someone to understand the therapeutic approach without becoming visually overwhelming. Every section was designed to guide the visitor gently toward making contact or booking a consultation.

03 / STORYTELLING

Turning a biography into a journey.

Instead of presenting the therapist's background as a conventional block of text, the website uses a visual timeline to make her story easier and more engaging to explore.

The vertical structure creates a natural rhythm and reinforces the feeling of movement, growth and personal development.

DESIGN DIRECTION

The identity was designed around an introspective and astral visual language — deep, quiet and reassuring.

04

Building a visual language around peace & reflection.

01 / COLOUR

Deep tones

Dark navy creates depth and stability, while muted gold introduces warmth, guidance and a subtle astral quality.

02 / TYPOGRAPHY

Soft contrast

Large serif typography creates emotional presence while restrained supporting text keeps the experience clear and readable.

03 / MOTION

Slow rhythm

Subtle movement and generous spacing encourage slower navigation and help create a feeling of calm rather than urgency.

05 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

Quiet, spacious and focused.

Circular forms reference cycles, introspection and balance. Combined with restrained colour and large areas of negative space, they reinforce the feeling of calm throughout the experience.

06

Designed to move from understanding to connection.

The website gives visitors a clear path: understand the therapeutic approach, explore the therapist's background, learn about the services and eventually make contact.

Booking and consultation information remain easy to reach without turning the experience into something overly commercial.
