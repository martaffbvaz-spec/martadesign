PROJECT 01 / DEEP AWARENESS

Deep Awareness.

A calm and immersive digital experience designed for an Ericksonian hypnotherapy practice.

WEB DESIGN / DEVELOPMENT PORTUGAL / 2026
01

A digital space designed to feel calm, human & intentional.

Deep Awareness is a website created for an Ericksonian hypnotherapy practice, with the goal of communicating trust, presence and emotional clarity.

The visual direction combines deep navy tones, soft typography and subtle movement to create a slower and more contemplative digital experience.

02 / STRUCTURE

Turning information into a journey.

Rather than presenting the practitioner through a conventional biography, the experience uses a visual timeline to turn her background into a story.

Small details, generous spacing and controlled contrast help keep the experience calm and easy to follow.

DESIGN DIRECTION

The aim was not to make the website feel clinical — but to create a space where someone could pause and reconnect.

03 / VISUAL LANGUAGE

Quiet, spacious and focused.

Large typography, restrained colour and circular elements reinforce the sense of reflection and continuity throughout the website.
